# Anvesh Chintala

**Building AI agents and automation systems that work end-to-end.**

I design and ship systems that connect language models to real workflows — multimodal data pipelines, intelligent job-matching agents, automated content engines. My work sits at the intersection of product thinking and applied AI engineering.

---

## Featured Projects

### [JobPilot AI Agent](https://github.com/chintalaanvesh/jobpilot-ai-agent) · [Live Demo](https://jobpilot-ai-agent.vercel.app)
AI-powered job search platform. Upload a resume, set preferences — the agent discovers jobs, scores each one 0–100 against your profile, and generates personalized cover letters and outreach emails.

`Next.js 14` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `Claude API` `n8n`

---

### [Gig Surge Analytics](https://github.com/chintalaanvesh/gig-surge-analytics)
Multimodal pipeline for quick-commerce delivery riders. GPT-4o Vision OCRs ephemeral surge incentive screenshots, structures the data into PostgreSQL, and exposes a conversational agent for natural-language earnings queries.

`n8n` `GPT-4o Vision` `PostgreSQL` `Google Sheets`

---

### [Instagram Content Agent](https://github.com/chintalaanvesh/instagram-content-agent)
Fully automated content pipeline — from RSS discovery and LLM-scored relevance ranking to AI-generated visuals and a scheduled content calendar. Zero manual input from idea to post.

`n8n` `GPT-4o` `Gemini Imagen 4.0` `Cloudinary`

---

### [Zomato Spend Analyzer](https://github.com/chintalaanvesh/zomato-spend-analyzer)
Automated food spend tracker. Extracts invoices, detects spending patterns, and delivers budget alerts via Telegram.

`n8n` `OpenAI` `Telegram` `Google Sheets`
